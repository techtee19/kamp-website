Switching to the flame

Both variants are already built and sitting in design/favicons/. Swapping is three copies — then hard-reload, because browsers cache favicons aggressively.

cd kamp-website
cp design/favicons/flame.ico      app/favicon.ico
cp design/favicons/flame-512.png  app/icon.png
cp design/favicons/flame-180.png  app/apple-icon.png

Or just say the word and I'll do it — and if you want the torch at large sizes with the flame at 16 and 32, that is a normal thing to do and I can build the icon that way instead.
